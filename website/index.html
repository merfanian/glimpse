Installing from the GitHub download (Chrome / Edge)
  1. Download glimpse-chrome-edge-<version>.zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions) and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.

Then, in three steps

  1. 1
    Open a paper

    In Overleaf or any PDF. For a PDF in the browser's own viewer, accept the Open in Glimpse suggestion.

  2. 2
    Hover a citation

    A Preview button appears next to it. Click it.

  3. 3
    Read, cite, keep going

    TL;DR and abstract instantly, Full PDF in one click, Cite to copy BibTeX.

Your reading stays yours

Glimpse has no servers, accounts, analytics or tracking. When you preview a citation, only that one bibliography entry is sent — directly from your browser — to public scholarly databases (Semantic Scholar, arXiv, Crossref, OpenAlex). Read the privacy policy →

Questions

Why doesn't Glimpse work inside Chrome's or Firefox's own PDF viewer?

Browsers don't let extensions run inside their built-in PDF viewers. So when you open a PDF, Glimpse offers to open it in its own viewer, where previews work. Choose Always in the toolbar popup to skip the question.

Which papers work?

Any PDF whose citations are links to the bibliography — nearly every paper written in LaTeX, including arXiv papers. Glimpse handles numbered and author–year styles, two-column layouts and review copies with line numbers.

Why do some papers show only the abstract?

The full-PDF preview needs an open-access copy (for example on arXiv). Otherwise Glimpse shows the abstract and an Open link to the paper's page.

What if Glimpse picks the wrong paper?

Every match shows a confidence score, and you can switch to other matches. When nothing matches clearly, Glimpse says so and offers possible matches and a Google Scholar search instead of guessing.

Does it cost anything?

No. Glimpse is free and open source under the MIT license.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Open an issue on GitHub. A link to the PDF you were reading helps a lot.
